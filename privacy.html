Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026Terms of Use

Contents

OpenBeast is a notebook app for macOS. It has no accounts and collects no usage statistics. This policy explains what this website and the OpenBeast app send over the network, and to whom.

1. This website

  • The site is static and hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard access logs (such as your IP address, browser and the pages you open) to run and protect the service.
  • The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ads.
  • To show the latest version number, your browser asks GitHub (api.github.com) for it. The download buttons take you to the installer on GitHub.

2. The OpenBeast app

  • No account, and no usage statistics, analytics or crash reporting. Your docs, apps and conversations stay on your Mac.
  • The app goes online only to check for and download updates (through openbeast.app, with the files coming from GitHub), to load the app list and the apps you install when you open the App Store page (every package is signature-checked), and, while you look at the AI tool list in Settings, to look up the newest versions of those tools and, when you ask, the public list of AI providers.
  • Things you start yourself, such as importing from GitHub, talk to the server you point them at.

3. AI

OpenBeast does not sell AI and does not sit between you and it. The AI tools you install sign in with your own accounts, and your requests go from your Mac straight to their providers, never through an OpenBeast server. Each provider’s own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

4. Optional phone features

Linking a phone (Settings → Phone) is optional. If you do, your Mac syncs the content you choose (to-dos, docs, chats and their attachments, and the app pages you open on the phone) with the relay service you sign up for, which stores it so your phone can reach it. That service has its own privacy policy and terms, shown before you sign up.

5. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date appear on this page. If you have a question, ask us on GitHub.

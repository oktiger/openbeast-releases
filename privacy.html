北京最焦科技有限公司 (“we”) operates OpenBeast’s remote service (“Remote”): the web version at remote.openbeast.cn and the iOS app “OpenBeast”. This policy explains what personal information we process when you use Remote, why we process it, where it is stored, and how you can manage it.

The OpenBeast desktop app itself needs no account and does not upload your docs, apps or conversations to us. This policy applies only once you sign up for a Remote account and link your Mac under Settings → Phone.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your phone number, if you sign in with one; your email address and password, if you sign up with email (the password is stored only as an irreversible hash); if you sign in with WeChat, the user identifier and nickname provided by the WeChat Open Platform.
  • Device information: the device name, system version, OpenBeast version and last-used time of the phones and browsers you sign in on and of the Macs you link; your push token, if you turn on notifications.
  • Content you sync: conversations synced through Remote (messages, the AI’s working steps, attachments and images), copies of your to-dos and docs, app page files uploaded by your Mac, and the names and results of shortcuts. So that it can be passed between your phone and your Mac, this content is stored on our servers, currently in plain text, without end-to-end encryption.
  • Security records: visitor IP addresses and request times, kept for a short time to prevent abuse (such as repeatedly requesting verification codes).

2. Why we use this information

  • To sign you in and verify your identity (by sending SMS or email verification codes), and to confirm it is you before your account is deleted;
  • To sync and pass content between your phone and your Mac, so your phone can use the AI on your Mac;
  • To notify you when an AI reply is finished or needs your decision;
  • To limit request rates, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use your content to train any model. The AI runs on your own Mac with your own accounts; our servers do not call any AI and do not store any AI keys.

3. Who we share it with

  • Alibaba Cloud: provides our servers and sends SMS and email (within mainland China). When we send a verification code, your phone number or email address is passed to it.
  • WeChat Open Platform (Tencent): used only if you choose to sign in with WeChat, to confirm your WeChat identity.
  • Apple Push Notification service: used only if you turn on notifications in the iOS app, to deliver notifications to your phone.

Apart from these, we do not provide your personal information to third parties unless laws and regulations require it.

4. Where it is stored and for how long

  • Data is stored on servers in mainland China (Alibaba Cloud China East 1, Hangzhou) and does not leave the country.
  • Account information is kept for as long as the account exists; attachments and images in conversations are deleted automatically after 30 days; verification codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete all of that account’s data on our servers: the account, devices, conversations and attachments, copies of to-dos and docs, images and invite codes. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

5. Your rights

  • View and manage: see your account, signed-in devices and storage usage in the app’s Settings; sign out any phone under Settings → Phone on your Mac.
  • Stop syncing: turn off the matching sync switch on your Mac, or unlink the Mac; its copies on the server are deleted as well.
  • Delete your account: “Delete account” at the bottom of the app’s Settings; once you confirm it is you, all data is deleted immediately.
  • Turn off notifications: in the app’s Settings or in your system settings.

6. Minors

Minors under the age of 14 should use Remote only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian.

7. Policy updates and contacting us

If this policy changes in an important way, we will point it out prominently in the app or on this page. If you have any question about this policy or your personal information, email support@openbeast.cn; we will reply within 15 working days.

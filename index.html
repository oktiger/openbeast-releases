Your notebook, with your own AI

OpenBeast is a notebook for macOS: your docs and small apps live together, and the AI command-line tools you already use (Claude Code, Codex and others) write and edit them for you.

Download for Mac Apple silicon Intel

Free · Beta · Latest · Requires macOS 13 or later.
Copies downloaded here update themselves in the background.

Not sure which chip your Mac has?

Click the Apple menu at the top left → “About This Mac”. If it says “Chip Apple M…”, pick Apple silicon; if it says “Processor Intel…”, pick Intel.
